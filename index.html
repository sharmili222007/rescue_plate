WHY RESCUE PLATE ✳

Good food should
find good people.

Every day, perfectly good food can go uneaten while families and communities need support. Rescue Plate makes it easier for food donors to share surplus food with NGOs that can help distribute it responsibly.

Discover how it works ↗
01 / DONORS
🥗

Give food a second life.

Restaurants, hotels, events and households can list safe surplus food for donation.

02 / NGO PARTNERS
🤝

Bring people together.

NGOs can discover nearby food donations and coordinate collection with donors.

03 / COMMUNITIES
💛

Make kindness count.

More rescued food means more opportunities to support people in the community.
